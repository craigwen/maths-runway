What she should be able to do (Year 3)

Number: count in 4s, 8s, 50s, 100s; read, write and order numbers to 1000; add and subtract 3-digit numbers (column method); recall the 3, 4 and 8 times tables.

Later this year: fractions, measures (length, mass, money, time), shape and statistics.

School focus right now is usually place value, then addition and subtraction (autumn term).
